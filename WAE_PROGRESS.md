# WAE Progress

Statuses: NOT STARTED, IN PROGRESS, IMPLEMENTED, TESTED, BLOCKED.

**TESTED** means automated tests exist and passed on the last run (145 backend tests on PostgreSQL, 141 on SQLite where 4 PostgreSQL-only tests are skipped, 2026-11-18, including checks that every API operation documents a typed response and the error envelope, the data retention sweeps, and that event triggers and quota checks issue a fixed number of queries however large an organization grows). It does **not** mean verified against live external services or PostgreSQL; those gaps are listed per module and in WAE_IMPLEMENTATION_REPORT.md. Frontend: 38 Playwright browser tests cover the builder, invitations and password reset, templates and packages, agents and knowledge bases, integrations, role-based navigation, analytics, billing and plans, audit, executions (retry, resume), notifications and staff invitations, two-factor sign-in, and that every main screen opens (72/72 over three repeats in Edge, 2026-11-12; 34/34 after the builder comfort features, the new engine steps, the retention screen, the Mailbox Trigger, the AI Marketing Team and content calendar, the phone and tablet layouts and the Content-Security-Policy were added). Every screen is checked to fit 390 px and 768 px wide without sideways scrolling; below 1024 px the sidebar is a drawer and the builder's step list and settings become a drawer and a bottom sheet. Screens are not checked for visual design.
